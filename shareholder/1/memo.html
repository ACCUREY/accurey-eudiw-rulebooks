1. DocType and namespace

The DocType of the shareholder attestation shall be com.accurey.shareholder.1. The attestation uses a single namespace, com.accurey.shareholder.1, and is encoded in mdoc (ISO/IEC 18013-5). An equivalent SD-JWT VC type is published at https://vct.accurey.com/shareholder/1 with the same semantics (nested company object).

It attests that a natural person holds a shareholding in a given company, according to the share register maintained on the Accurey platform, as of an explicit reference date — without revealing the size of the holding unless the verifier asks for the band. Legal category: non-qualified EAA (eIDAS 2).

No identity attribute is carried in the attestation. The link with the holder is established (a) by device key binding to the wallet that received the attestation and (b) by presenting the attestation together with a PID when the verifier needs the holder's identity; the verifier then matches on PID attributes.

4. Trust and validity

  • MSO signed with an Accurey Document Signer certificate (EKU 1.0.18013.5.1.2, P-256, ES256) chained to CN=Accurey IACA, O=ACCUREY, C=LU, uploaded to the EUDIW Unfold Trusted List (Trust Management Platform of the Interop Week).
  • Validity: validUntil = signed + 48 h; the attestation is short-lived and re-issued from the register on demand. Revocation lists are therefore not used in this version.
  • Issuance: OpenID4VCI, pre-authorized code with tx_code, proof types jwt and attestation; credential configuration ids acc_shareholder_mdoc and acc_shareholder_sd_jwt.
  • Presentation: OpenID4VP 1.0, DCQL, response_mode direct_post or direct_post.jwt, client_id scheme x509_san_dns.
